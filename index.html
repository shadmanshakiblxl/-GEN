Detailed Architecture

Three Interconnected Bio Modules

Operating seamlessly across all biological scales—from organismal field surveillance to mutation mechanics and structure-based drug discovery.

01 / 03
Computer Vision & Field Sentinel

&GEN Biological Intelligence

Bio Analyzer Module

A multi-modal computer vision and bio-surveillance engine designed for real-time field identification. The module automatically segments, classifies, and evaluates specimens across all biological tiers—from full organisms (e.g. Scolopendra subspinipes) to microscopic tissue slides (e.g. Iris germanica).

1. Multi-Class Biological Segmentation

Detects species, cell types, tissues, organs, and organelles with high-confidence accuracy scoring. Runs edge-optimized multi-scale convolutional neural networks to delineate individual tergite trunk segments, appendage counts, and histological layer boundaries.

2. SENTINEL Hazard & Biochemical Flags

Identifies active toxins (e.g., Ssm Spooky Toxin, Phospholipase A2, neurotoxic KCNQ blockers) and automatically issues instantaneous safety, handling, and envenomation protocols to prevent field hazardous exposure.

3. Genomic & Evolutionary Profiling

Maps physical visual observations directly to genetic markers ($COI$, $16S\text{ rRNA}$) and historical taxonomy lineages for rapid taxonomic classification without field lab sequencing delay.

02 / 03
Translational Genomics

&GEN Genomic Mapper

Variant Impact Explorer

A translational engine for evaluating genetic mutations and their downstream structural and physiological consequences across genomic loci and protein domains.

1. Multi-Database Variant Scoring

Integrates state-of-the-art pathogenicity metrics (AlphaMissense: 0.984, CADD: 34.0, REVEL: 0.942) for single nucleotide variants and missense mutations such as TP53 c.818G>A (p.Arg273His).

2. Transcription Factor & Splice-Site Analysis

Quantifies promoter binding affinity loss ($\Delta\text{Affinity}: -12.7\text{ bits}$) and evaluates splice donor/acceptor site disruptions using SpliceAI deep neural networks.

3. 3D Structural Stability Mechanics

Renders atomic 3D protein structures (e.g. PDB: 1TUP) to compute thermodynamic folding free energy changes ($\Delta\Delta G = -2.85\text{ kcal/mol}$), lost hydrogen bond networks, steric clashes, and altered solvent exposure.

03 / 03
Computational Chemistry

&GEN DRUG

Structure-Based Virtual Screening

A computational chemistry tool for rational ligand design, active pocket preparation, virtual screening, and nano-molar binding kinetics prediction.

1. Target-Ligand Docking

Simulates atomic interactions between target receptor catalytic binding clefts (e.g., HIV-1 Protease, PDB: 1HSG) and small-molecule candidate libraries formatted via SMILES strings.

2. Affinity & Kinetic Scoring

Calculates total free binding energy ($\Delta G = -11.96\text{ kcal/mol}$) and accurately predicts binding kinetic constants: dissociation constant ($K_d: 1.71\text{ nM}$), inhibition constant ($K_i: 1.85\text{ nM}$), and half-maximal inhibitory concentration ($\text{IC}_{50}: 3.70\text{ nM}$).

3. Pharmacokinetics & ADME Validation

Evaluates Lipinski’s Rule of 5 drug-likeness compliance, molecular weight, LogP lipophilicity, ligand efficiency metrics, and active-site hydrogen-bond networks.

&GEN, a unified, mobile-first bio-computational platform developed by Shadman Shakib. &GEN bridges macro-scale biological observation with micro-scale molecular modeling, integrating field bio-detection, genomic variant mapping, and structure-based drug discovery into an end-to-end digital suite.

The platform is built on three interconnected modules that operate across every level of biological complexity:

1. &GEN Biological Intelligence (Bio Analyzer)

A multi-modal computer vision and bio-surveillance engine designed for real-time field identification. The module automatically segments, classifies, and evaluates specimens across all biological tiers—from full organisms (Scolopendra subspinipes) to microscopic tissue slides (Iris germanica):

  • Multi-Class Biological Segmentation: Detects species, cell types, tissues, organs, and organelles with confidence scoring.
  • SENTINEL Hazard & Biochemical Flags: Identifies active toxins (e.g., Ssm Spooky Toxin, Phospholipase A2) and automatically issues safety and envenomation protocols.
  • Genomic & Evolutionary Profiling: Maps observations to gene markers ($COI$, $16S\text{ rRNA}$) and historical lineage classifications.
2. &GEN Genomic Mapper (Variant Impact Explorer)

A translational engine for evaluating genetic mutations and their structural consequences:

  • Multi-Database Variant Scoring: Integrates pathogenicity metrics (AlphaMissense: 0.984, CADD: 34.0, REVEL: 0.942) for mutations like TP53 c.818G>A.
  • Transcription Factor & Splice-Site Analysis: Quantifies promoter binding loss ($\Delta\text{Affinity}: -12.7\text{ bits}$) and evaluates splicing disruptions via SpliceAI.
  • 3D Structural Stability: Renders atomic 3D protein structures (PDB: 1TUP) to calculate folding energy changes ($\Delta\Delta G = -2.85\text{ kcal/mol}$), lost hydrogen bonds, and solvent exposure.
3. &GEN DRUG (Structure-Based Virtual Screening)

A computational chemistry tool for rational ligand design and binding kinetics:

  • Target-Ligand Docking: Simulates interactions between target receptors (e.g., HIV-1 Protease, PDB: 1HSG) and small molecules via SMILES input.
  • Affinity & Kinetic Scoring: Calculates free binding energy ($\Delta G = -11.96\text{ kcal/mol}$) and predicts binding constants ($K_d: 1.71\text{ nM}$, $K_i: 1.85\text{ nM}$, $\text{IC}_{50}: 3.7\text{ nM}$).
  • Pharmacokinetics & ADME: Evaluates Lipinski’s Rule of 5 compliance, ligand efficiency metrics, and active-site hydrogen-bond networks.

By combining field identification, variant analysis, and molecular docking into a single mobile application, &GEN reduces the latency between biological discovery and computational insight.
